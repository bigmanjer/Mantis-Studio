# Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what MANTIS Studio collects, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. What we collect
- Account details: your email address and an encrypted password (handled by our authentication provider).
- Your Content: projects, chapters, outlines, notes and World Bible entries you create.
- Billing details: your plan, subscription status and a Stripe customer ID. Card details are entered directly with Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
- AI settings: your chosen provider and model, and — if you add one — your own API key, which we encrypt before storing.
- Usage data: how many AI tokens you've used each month (to apply plan limits), plus basic server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and debugging.

## 2. How we use it
To provide the Service; to save and sync your work; to process payments and apply plan limits; to run AI features you request; to keep the Service secure; and to contact you about your account, billing or important changes. We do not sell your personal information, show you third-party ads, or use your writing to train AI models.

## 3. Who we share it with
We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

- Supabase — database and authentication (stores your account and Your Content).
- Vercel — website hosting.
- Stripe — payment processing.
- Groq or OpenAI — only the text needed for an AI request you make. When you use your own key, the request goes to the provider you chose under your account.
- An email delivery provider — for sign-up confirmation and password reset emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

## 4. Cookies
We use only essential cookies to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 5. Retention
We keep your account and content while your account is open. When you delete your account, your content is permanently deleted from our live database straight away and from backups within 30 days. We keep billing records as long as tax law requires.

## 6. Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so each user can only read their own projects, and stored API keys are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 7. Your rights
You can view, edit and export your content at any time, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, or object to certain processing. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll respond within 30 days.

## 8. International transfers
Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 9. Children
The Service is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Changes
If we make material changes we'll notify you by email or in the app before they take effect.
